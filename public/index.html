Why keep your most important secrets off every computer?

  1. Anything on a computer can be copied without you noticing. Malware, a synced folder, a phone backup, a screenshot, a clipboard manager - a digital secret leaks silently, and you find out months later. A sheet of paper has to be physically taken.
  2. A password manager is right for almost everything - except the keys to it. The master password, a crypto seed phrase, two-factor recovery codes: those cannot live inside the thing they unlock, and they are exactly the ones a thief wants.
  3. "Written down" should not mean "readable". Whoever finds the sheet should see meaningless marks. That is what an encryption tool is: a grid, a pad, a square or a rule that turns your writing into noise for everyone but you.
  4. Paper fails differently. Fire, damp, house moves, forgetting. So the tools here come with honest labels for what they do and do not defeat, and the instructions cover keeping the paper alive.

This page makes you an encryption tool, a password, or both - five results each time. Everything is generated on this device with the browser's cryptographic random number generator, shown once, stored nowhere, sent nowhere. It works with the network unplugged.
